1. About this beta

MealMate is a small, experimental meal-planning and community-recipe service. Features may change, become temporarily unavailable, or contain errors as we improve the app. This beta is intended for invited adult testers; please do not use it as a source of professional nutrition or medical advice.

2. Your account

You agree to provide an email address you control, protect your password and account access, and avoid using another person's account without permission. Report suspected unauthorized access to the contact address below. Account verification and password recovery are handled through Supabase Auth.

3. Your kitchen and recipes

Your unpublished meal plans, shopping lists, chores, and private recipes are intended to stay private to your account. You retain your rights in your original recipes. When you publish a recipe to the Community Cookbook, you permit MealMate to store, display, and make that post available to other signed-in users for the operation of the community feature. You may unshare a recipe later, although other users' previously saved personal copies may remain.

4. Respect others’ work

Only share recipes and other content you have permission to share. If you save another cook's community recipe, you may use and edit your private copy, but MealMate's current beta does not permit republishing that copy as an original community post. Do not impersonate another cook or falsely claim someone else's work as yours.

5. Food safety

Recipes and comments are supplied by users and are not professionally safety-reviewed. Check ingredients for allergies and dietary needs; follow proper washing, cooking temperatures, storage, and appliance instructions. MealMate's recipe examples are for convenience and learning, not guaranteed results.

6. Acceptable use

Do not post spam, threats, harassment, hate or discriminatory content, sexual content, malicious links, private information about others, unsafe instructions presented as safe, or material that infringes someone else's rights. Do not attempt to bypass account restrictions or disrupt the service.

7. Moderation and reports

We may review reports manually and remove public posts or restrict accounts when needed to keep the beta safe. Reporting a recipe does not automatically remove it. We cannot promise continuous moderation or a specific response time during the beta.

8. Availability and limits

MealMate is provided on a beta, as-available basis. Keep backups of information you cannot afford to lose. Services and data can be affected by outages, internet connection problems, sync conflicts, or mistakes. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or protections that cannot legally be waived.

9. Ending use and contacting us

You can stop using the service and request account-data removal by emailing kyoku.mealmate@gmail.com. We may suspend accounts that seriously breach community rules or pose a security risk. See the Privacy Notice for how deletion requests are handled.

10. Changes

We may revise these terms as the beta grows. We will update the date at the top of this page when changes are published. Please review changes before continuing to use the service.

Questions? Email kyoku.mealmate@gmail.com. These pages describe the current MealMate friends-only beta and should be reviewed as the service grows.
