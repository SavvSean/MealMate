Community Guidelines

A friendly cooking corner works best when everyone helps.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

On this page

  • Be welcoming and constructive
  • Share recipes responsibly
  • Keep dishes reasonably safe
  • Protect privacy
  • No spam or harmful content
  • Reporting and moderation
  • Unsharing and copies
  • Help keep the kitchen improving

Be welcoming and constructive

MealMate is a small cooking space for beginners and home cooks. Encourage questions, share practical tips, and be respectful even if someone cooks differently from you. Harassment, bullying, threats, and hate are not welcome.

Share recipes responsibly

Publish recipes you created or have permission to share. Give appropriate credit for adaptations. Do not upload someone else's personal or copyrighted work as your own. In the current beta, personal copies of other members' recipes cannot be republished from MealMate's normal Share action.

Keep dishes reasonably safe

Include clear ingredients and cooking steps. Avoid implying that raw chicken, unsafe storage, inappropriate equipment use, or other food hazards are safe. Community submissions are not professionally reviewed: every cook should verify cooking temperatures, allergies, and their own equipment instructions.

Protect privacy

Do not put anyone's email, phone number, precise address, credentials, or private kitchen data in a public chef name or recipe. Your public chef name and recipes are visible to signed-in members, so choose what you share carefully.

No spam or harmful content

Do not post repetitive promotions, scams, malicious links, impersonation, hateful material, sexually explicit content, or instructions intended to cause harm. Keep community posts focused on meals and cooking.

Reporting and moderation

Use the Report option on a community recipe and select the closest reason (unsafe, spam, inappropriate, or other). Reports are stored for manual review; they do not automatically hide or delete content. For urgent concerns or mistaken reports, contact kyoku.mealmate@gmail.com. Moderation capacity is limited during the friends-only beta.

Unsharing and copies

You may unshare your original public post at any time. That removes the community post, not the private original in your Cookbook. Other users may still have previously saved a personal copy; those copies are not the same as ownership of your original recipe.

Help keep the kitchen improving

If something feels confusing, broken, or unsafe, tell us. The beta is for learning what genuinely helps people cook and shop with less hassle—not for collecting perfect-looking recipes.

Questions? Email kyoku.mealmate@gmail.com. These pages describe the current MealMate friends-only beta and should be reviewed as the service grows.
