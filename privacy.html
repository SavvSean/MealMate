1. Who runs MealMate

MealMate is an independent kitchen-planning web application currently offered as a small, friends-only beta by its developer in the Philippines. For privacy questions, account-data requests, or concerns, email kyoku.mealmate@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, Supabase account identifier, and authentication/session information. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; MealMate does not intentionally keep your password in its kitchen-data table.
  • Your private kitchen: meals and schedules, completion status, grocery lists and prices, budgets, little chores, personal recipes, optional public chef display name, and other app settings.
  • Community contributions: recipes you choose to publish, ingredients, instructions, public chef display name, and timestamps. These are visible to other signed-in MealMate users.
  • Reports: a report of a community recipe, the reason you select, and your account identifier for handling duplicate reports and moderation.
  • Browser data: MealMate keeps session information and may cache kitchen data, tutorial preferences, or recovery copies in your browser's local storage.
  • Technical data: our hosting and authentication providers may process connection, device, log, and security information needed to operate their services.

3. Why we use this information

We process information to create and secure accounts; save and synchronize meals, groceries, tasks, and recipes; send verification and password-recovery messages; display recipes that users explicitly choose to share; review community reports; troubleshoot errors; and maintain the service. We aim to collect only what these functions need.

4. What is private and what is shared

Your personal kitchen, including shopping lists, budgets, chores, meal plans, and unpublished recipes, is intended to be accessible only through your account. When you choose Share recipe, the published recipe and your chosen public chef name become visible to other signed-in users. Your account email is not shown as your public chef name. Saving a public recipe as a personal copy does not grant permission to republish it as your own. If someone saved a private copy of your shared recipe before you unshared it, that copy may remain in their personal cookbook.

5. Service providers and transfers

MealMate relies on Cloudflare Pages for website hosting, Supabase for account authentication and data storage, and a dedicated Gmail SMTP account to deliver authentication emails. These providers may process information using infrastructure outside the Philippines under their applicable policies and security controls. We do not use Resend to deliver emails in this current beta configuration. We have not intentionally added advertising trackers or a separate product-analytics service to this beta; hosting providers may still keep standard technical logs. This notice will be updated if analytics are enabled.

6. Security

We use HTTPS on the hosted site, Supabase authentication, and database Row Level Security rules to restrict access to private kitchen data and authors' community posts. We have tested several permission boundaries, but no system can promise perfect security. Use a unique password and avoid adding sensitive personal details to recipes or chore descriptions.

7. How long information is kept

Your account and saved kitchen information normally remain while your account is active. Published community posts remain until you unshare them, the account is removed, or a moderator removes them. Reports may be kept as needed to review safety and misuse. On a verified deletion request, we will arrange deletion of the relevant account and associated application records, subject to legal and operational limits; provider backups or logs may persist for a limited time according to provider retention practices. We do not yet offer an automatic in-app account-deletion control.

8. Your choices and rights

You can edit your own kitchen and personal recipes, change your public chef name, unshare your published recipes, and sign out. To request access, correction, export assistance, or deletion of account information, contact kyoku.mealmate@gmail.com. Requests may require verifying that you own the account. Data subjects in the Philippines may also have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, including access, correction, erasure or blocking where applicable, and complaints to the National Privacy Commission.

9. Children and sensitive information

This limited beta is intended for invited adult testers. Please do not create an account if you are under 18. Avoid entering medical diagnoses, passwords, payment details, precise addresses, or other sensitive information in recipe notes, chores, chef names, or community content.

10. Updates and contact

We may revise this notice as MealMate changes. The date above will change when we publish an updated version. For questions about how data is handled, email kyoku.mealmate@gmail.com.

Questions? Email kyoku.mealmate@gmail.com. These pages describe the current MealMate friends-only beta and should be reviewed as the service grows.
